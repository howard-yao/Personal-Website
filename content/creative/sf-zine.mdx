## About this series

I love street photography! What motivates me to pursue photography is the desire to document the very moments that make up my life. I don't usually go out of my way to stage experiences or chase beautiful scenes just for the sake of a photograph. But let moments find me.

It allows me to enjoy life and follow my curiosity freely, while also encouraging me to slow down, observe, feel, and appreciate the beauty and little miracles that already exist around me. It may not be the philosophy of photography that most people share, but it is the one that feels the most genuine to me.

This series was photographed around April 2026. Experimented with zine inspired elements into their presentation.

I hope you enjoy it (❁´◡`❁)
